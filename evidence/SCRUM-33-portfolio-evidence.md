## Screenshot Quality

Screenshots should be readable at normal document size.

Crop unnecessary desktop/browser areas where appropriate, but retain enough context to show what system or platform is being evidenced.

Do not include unrelated personal browser tabs, email addresses, notifications or other private information if they are not required for the assessment.

For application screenshots, use realistic but non-sensitive test data.
